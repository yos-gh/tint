TINT

TINT is not Tetris
Source

TINT is a physics-driven falling-block puzzle about piling up stones connected by elastic cords. Pieces bend, bounce, and rotate freely. Connect both walls with stones at roughly the same height to clear the stones along that line.

Controls

  • Move: A / D or left/right arrows
  • Soft drop: S or down arrow
  • Rotate counterclockwise: N
  • Rotate clockwise: M
  • Restart after game over: R
  • Touchscreen: left-side D-pad and right-side rotation buttons

Clear Rule

A line clears when stones touch both walls at roughly the same height and enough stones fill the space around the segment between them. A line that needs only one more stone glows gold.

Scoring

Clearing higher in the field earns a larger risk multiplier: x1 near the floor and up to x16 near the game-over limit. The pile must stay above that limit for five continuous seconds to end the game.

Prototype

This prototype is built with Godot 4. Physics, difficulty, and clearing conditions may continue to change during development.
