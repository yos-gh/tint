Open the game on its own ↗

TINT is a physics-driven falling-block puzzle. Familiar tetromino shapes are built from soft, connected stones, so every piece bends, bounces, and rotates freely.

How to play

Goal

Connect both walls with stones at roughly the same height to clear that line. A line one stone away from clearing glows gold.

Scoring

Clearing higher in the field earns a larger multiplier, from x1 near the floor up to x16 near the height limit.

Game over

The game ends when the pile stays above the height limit for five continuous seconds.
